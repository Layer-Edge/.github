# LayerEdge: Revolutionizing Bitcoin with Decentralized Scalability  

## 🌐 What Is LayerEdge?  
LayerEdge is the **first decentralized network** designed to enhance Bitcoin’s Blockspace with **ZK (Zero-Knowledge)** and **BitVM** technologies. By leveraging these innovations, LayerEdge ensures every layer of computation is **securely anchored to Bitcoin**, delivering scalability and cost efficiency like never before.  

👉 **How does LayerEdge work?**  
Often, efficient settlement on Bitcoin relies on enabling `OP_Cat` on Bitcoin’s L1. With or without `OP_Cat`, LayerEdge revolutionizes the process by enabling **shared transactions** and **proof aggregation**, providing unmatched performance while maintaining Bitcoin's security standards.

## 🛠 Why Are We Building This?  

Bitcoin’s unmatched security comes at a cost:  
- Limited scripting capabilities restrict advanced functionalities.  
- High costs make ZK-proof verification economically unfeasible, leaving L2 solutions struggling with scalability or sustainability.  

**LayerEdge bridges this gap** by enabling:  
- **Proof Aggregation**: Protocols share verification costs, significantly reducing expenses.  
- **Economic Viability**: Transforming what was once financially restrictive into affordable solutions.  

Our vision is to make **Bitcoin’s security accessible to all protocols**, empowering innovation across the ecosystem.

## 🚀 How Does It Work?  

LayerEdge introduces an **optimized modular design** for computation:  
- **ZK Proofs**: Shift complex computations off-chain and verify them on-chain with concise proofs.  
- **BitVM**: Extend Bitcoin’s capabilities while preserving its decentralization.  

This approach mirrors Ethereum's rollups but without the fragmentation, ensuring a seamless, scalable ecosystem.  

**Efficiency Highlights:**  
- Proof verification costs on Bitcoin drop from **$900+** to **less than $20** per protocol in networks with 50+ protocols.  
- Shared costs reduce expenses as more protocols join LayerEdge, fostering a network effect for scalability.
